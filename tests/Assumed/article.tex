A test of a definition whose well-definedness is not proved. The proofs of this article
have no proof that the function below is well defined, so the certificate assumes it,
names the assumption among its axioms, and the report says so.

Define $f:(0,\infty)\to\mathbb{R}$ by $f(x)\coloneqq x+\frac1x$.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:value}
For every $x\in(0,\infty)$, $f(x)=x+\frac1x$.
\end{lemma}
