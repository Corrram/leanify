\section{Notions}

Let $\CC$ denote the class of bivariate copulas.
We call a copula \(C\) \emph{stochastically increasing} (\emph{SI}) if its Markov kernel has a version such that, for every \(v\in(0,1)\), the map \(t\mapsto K_C(t,[0,v])\) is non-increasing. We also use a classical tail notion. \(C\) is \emph{left-tail decreasing} (\emph{LTD}) if, for every \(v\in(0,1)\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ltd}
u\longmapsto\frac{C(u,v)}{u},\qquad u\in(0,1],
\end{equation}
is non-increasing.

\section{Using the notion}

\begin{proposition}
For every LTD copula $C$, $C(u,v)\ge uv$ for all $u,v\in[0,1]$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}
Let $C$ be an LTD copula. Then $C(u,v)\ge uv$ for all $u,v\in[0,1]$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}
If $C$ is an LTD copula, then $C(u,v)\ge uv$ for all $u,v\in[0,1]$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}
Every LTD copula $C$ satisfies $C(u,v)\ge uv$ for all $u,v\in[0,1]$.
\end{proposition}

\section{Properties of a class and of a quantity on it}

The words for these properties belong to the vocabulary of the unit square: the article names the field, and the field names their Lean meanings.
Define $\gamma:\CC\to\mathbb{R}$ by $\gamma(C)\coloneqq 4C(\frac12,\frac12)-1$.

\begin{proposition}
The class $\CC$ is convex, compact with respect to uniform convergence and closed with respect to uniform convergence.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}
The function $\gamma$ is convex, concave on $\CC$ and continuous with respect to uniform convergence.
\end{proposition}
